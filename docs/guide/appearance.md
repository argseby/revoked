# Appearance & accessibility

**Settings → Appearance and accessibility** controls how the app looks and
reads: the theme, how dates and times are written, the text size and
motion. Each choice takes effect immediately and shows its result on the
page. The settings are stored on the device, so each device keeps its own.

## Theme

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| **System** | Follows the device's light or dark setting (the default) |
| **Light** | Always light |
| **Dark** | Always dark |

## Dates and times

Each setting starts on **Automatic**, which follows the device's region and
time zone.

**Date format** lists each format with 31 December of the current year as
an example:

| Format | Example |
|---|---|
| Day, month, year with dots | 31.12.2026 |
| Day, month, year with slashes | 31/12/2026 |
| Month, day, year | 12/31/2026 |
| Year, month, day | 2026-12-31 |

**Automatic** picks one of these from the device's region, for example dots
in Germany and month first in the United States.

**Clock** offers **Automatic**, **24-hour** (14:05) and **12-hour**
(2:05 PM).

**Time zone** lists the device's own zone first, followed by UTC and every
regional zone by city. Below the field, the page shows the current time as
it will appear across the app.

## Text size

**App text size** scales text on top of the device's own text size, in
steps from 100% to 200%. At 100%, the app matches the device. A sample
grows as the slider moves.

## Motion

**Reduce motion** turns off fades and slides. It is always on when the
device itself requests reduced motion, whatever this switch says.
