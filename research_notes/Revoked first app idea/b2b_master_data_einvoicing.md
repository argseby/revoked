# B2B customer master data (billing address, VAT ID, IBAN, invoice e-mail, Peppol/Leitweg-ID) and the German/EU e-invoicing mandate — as of Sept 2026

## 1. German B2B e-invoicing mandate: timeline, formats, buyer data fields, consequences of wrong data

### Takeaway
Since 1 Jan 2025 every German business must be able to *receive* EN 16931 e-invoices; issuing becomes mandatory 1 Jan 2027 (prior-year turnover > EUR 800k) and 1 Jan 2028 for everyone. The mandate does not make buyer master data legally stricter in the VAT sense (buyer name/address remain the §14(4) UStG mandatory fields; a missing BT-10 Buyer reference is explicitly "VAT-irrelevant"), but it moves invoices into machine validation and automated delivery, so wrong or missing buyer data (electronic address, buyer reference, Leitweg-ID for public sector) now causes hard rejections instead of being silently fixed by a human.

### Cited Findings
- Primary source: BMF letter of 15 Oct 2025 (GZ III C 2 - S 7287-a/00019/007/243), "Einführung der obligatorischen elektronischen Rechnung bei Umsätzen zwischen inländischen Unternehmern ab dem 1. Januar 2025; Anpassung des UStAE", 33 pages; it supplements/replaces the first letter of Oct 2024 — [BMF PDF](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-15-einfuehrung-obligatorische-e-rechnung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3); [Haufe summary](https://www.haufe.de/finance/steuern-finanzen/bmf-schreiben-v-15102025-zur-e-rechnung_190_669628.html)
- Timeline: receiving mandatory for all domestic businesses from 1 Jan 2025, no transition; issuing: until 31 Dec 2026 paper/PDF still allowed (with recipient consent); until 31 Dec 2027 for businesses with prior-year turnover ≤ EUR 800,000; from 1 Jan 2028 all B2B. Exceptions: small-amount invoices ≤ EUR 250, tickets, Kleinunternehmer (§19) — [Baker Tilly on BMF letter 15.10.2025](https://www.bakertilly.de/beitrag/e-rechnung-zweites-bmf-schreiben-sorgt-fuer-mehr-klarheit); [DATEV](https://www.datev.de/web/de/berufsgruppenuebergreifend/themen-im-fokus/e-rechnung-mit-datev/gesetzliche-regelungen)
- Formats: only structured formats conforming to EN 16931 — XRechnung (UBL or CII syntax) and hybrid ZUGFeRD from v2.0.1 (except MINIMUM/BASIC-WL profiles); EDI allowed if data can be correctly extracted into EN 16931-compatible info; EDI transitional use until end-2027 — [BMF letter 15.10.2025, Abschn. 14.1 UStAE](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-15-einfuehrung-obligatorische-e-rechnung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3); [Sage](https://www.sage.com/de-de/blog/e-rechnung-pflicht-2027/)
- No consent needed: "Die Ausstellung einer E-Rechnung an einen anderen inländischen Unternehmer bedarf nicht der Zustimmung des Leistungsempfängers"; the recipient must create the technical preconditions for receipt — [BMF letter 15.10.2025, Abschn. 14.1 Abs. 2/5 UStAE](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-15-einfuehrung-obligatorische-e-rechnung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3)
- Transmission channel is free: e-invoices may be sent "per E-Mail, per Download über ein Internetportal oder per EDI, per Bereitstellung der Daten mittels einer elektronischen Schnittstelle oder durch gemeinsamen Zugriff auf einen zentralen Speicherort"; which format and channel the parties use "ist zivilrechtlich zwischen ihnen zu klären"; for e-mail receipt no separate mailbox is required — [BMF letter 15.10.2025](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-15-einfuehrung-obligatorische-e-rechnung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3). (Note: one vendor blog claims e-mail delivery is only allowed "until 31.12.2026" — this conflicts with the BMF letter and appears to confuse the PDF-transition rule with the e-mail channel — [kostenlose-erechnung.de via search](https://kostenlose-erechnung.de/ratgeber/peppol-rechnung-was-ist-das/).)
- If the recipient cannot technically receive or refuses the e-invoice, it has no right to a paper/other invoice; the issuer's obligations are deemed fulfilled if it demonstrably issued and attempted to transmit an e-invoice — [BMF letter 15.10.2025, Abschn. 14.1 Abs. 5](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-15-einfuehrung-obligatorische-e-rechnung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3)
- Three error classes (BMF 15.10.2025): (a) format errors — file violates EN 16931 syntax → it is not an e-invoice at all but a "sonstige Rechnung"; (b) business-rule errors (Geschäftsregelfehler), e.g. missing mandatory field "BT-10 Buyer reference" in an XRechnung, or tax amount inconsistent with rate, flagged as "critical error" by validators; (c) content errors (Inhaltsfehler) — violations of the §§14(4)/14a UStG mandatory VAT data — [BMF letter 15.10.2025, Rn. 6b and 35a](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-15-einfuehrung-obligatorische-e-rechnung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3)
- Key nuance for master data: "Geschäftsregelfehler zu anderen Inhalten (z. B. die fehlende Angabe des Feldes 'BT-10 Buyer reference') sind umsatzsteuerlich unbeachtlich." Only errors in the §14(4) UStG mandatory data (which include full name and address of the recipient, recipient VAT ID where reverse charge/intra-EU applies) make the invoice non-compliant (Abschn. 15.2a UStAE). Validation does not replace the recipient's duty to check invoices for completeness and correctness; keeping the validation report is recommended as evidence — [BMF letter 15.10.2025, Rn. 35a](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-15-einfuehrung-obligatorische-e-rechnung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3)
- Where an e-invoice is required but a "sonstige Rechnung" (e.g. plain PDF) is issued, it is "dem Grunde nach" not a proper invoice and does not entitle to input VAT deduction (with a grace provision in a new Abs. 1a) — [BMF letter 15.10.2025, Abschn. 15.2a Abs. 1 UStAE amendment](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-15-einfuehrung-obligatorische-e-rechnung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3)
- All VAT-mandatory data must be in the structured XML part; a reference to an attachment with the data in unstructured form is not sufficient — [BMF letter 15.10.2025](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-10-15-einfuehrung-obligatorische-e-rechnung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=3); [Baker Tilly](https://www.bakertilly.de/beitrag/e-rechnung-zweites-bmf-schreiben-sorgt-fuer-mehr-klarheit)
- Buyer electronic address: BT-49 "Buyer electronic address" is required by XRechnung 3.0.1+ (fits Peppol rule PEPPOL-EN16931-R010); for German public-sector buyers BT-49 typically uses scheme 0204 with the Leitweg-ID — [invoice-converter.com BT-49](https://www.invoice-converter.com/en/resources/xrechnung/bt-49-buyer-electronic-address); [KoSIT test-suite MR](https://projekte.kosit.org/xrechnung/xrechnung-testsuite/-/merge_requests/64/diffs?diff_id=14911&start_sha=83d2d57cfa20bc11380d7fb50ab7fdbaf889228f); [e-rechnung-bund.de: XRechnung 3.0.1 mandatory for federal invoicing](https://e-rechnung-bund.de/en/new-version-xrechnung-standard-3-0-1-available)
- Leitweg-ID / Peppol: for public institutions on the federal OZG-RE portal, the Peppol receiver ID equals the Leitweg-ID with prefix 0204 (e.g. 0204:99661-WEBSERVICEOZG-28); BT-10 carries only the Leitweg-ID itself — [e-rechnung-bund.de FAQ](https://e-rechnung-bund.de/en/faq/how-is-the-peppol-participant-id-connected-to-the-buyer-reference-leitweg-id/)
- Lexware Office's contact model has XRechnung-specific fields `buyerReference` (Leitweg-ID) and `vendorNumberAtCustomer` — i.e. per-customer master data an invoicing tool must hold to produce a valid XRechnung — [Lexware API docs](https://developers.lexware.io/docs/)
- Common rejection causes cited by practitioners: missing/ambiguous base data, incomplete mandatory fields, inconsistent master data (secondary, vendor blog) — [bonpago: XRechnung Fehlercodes](https://www.bonpago.de/blog/tag/e-rechnung/xrechnung-fehlercodes); [datex: E-Rechnung abgelehnt Gründe](https://www.datexgmbh.com/2025/12/e-rechnung-abgelehnt-gruende/)
- Readiness: Bitkom (survey Apr–Jun 2024, n=1,103 firms ≥20 employees, published 3 Dec 2024): only 45% could receive e-invoices; 55% used e-invoices for outgoing invoices (30% frequently, 25% occasionally); 96% receive invoices by e-mail, 99% send by e-mail (often PDF); preferred receiving formats EDI 71%, ZUGFeRD/Factur-X 27%, XRechnung 5% — [Bitkom press release](https://www.bitkom.org/Presse/Presseinformation/Weniger-als-die-Haelfte-deutscher-Unternehmen-empfaengt-E-Rechnungen)
- Later readiness data (secondary): Bitkom figure of 43% sending e-invoices as of March 2025; a June 2026 Quadient study: 75% consider processes digitised but only 6% meet legal requirements; a survey reported "only 24% ready" for 2027 — [Sage blog](https://www.sage.com/de-de/blog/e-rechnung-pflicht-2027/); [it-boltwise](https://www.it-boltwise.de/e-rechnung-2027-nur-24-fertig-umfrage-warnt-vor-umsetzungsdruck.html) (not verified against primary studies)
- Channel preference: in a VeR survey Peppol was by far the most-cited preferred transmission route; e-mail seen as an outgoing model (secondary) — [Sage blog](https://www.sage.com/de-de/blog/e-rechnung-pflicht-2027/)
- OpenPeppol: >1.4 million endpoints registered Europe-wide as of Q1 2026; 14 of 16 German Länder connected (secondary, not verified) — [kostenlose-erechnung.de](https://kostenlose-erechnung.de/ratgeber/peppol-rechnung-was-ist-das/)

### Inferences
- The mandate creates new per-customer master data that did not exist in the PDF world: the buyer's *electronic address* (BT-49: an e-mail or a Peppol participant ID with scheme), the *preferred channel/format* (XRechnung vs ZUGFeRD, Peppol vs e-mail vs portal — agreed "zivilrechtlich" per customer), and for public-sector customers the Leitweg-ID/buyer reference. Each of these must be learned from the customer and kept current by the supplier, i.e. exactly a "customer tells supplier, supplier needs the current value" problem.
- The hard-failure point is machine validation and routing (BT-49 missing → Peppol/XRechnung rejection; wrong Peppol ID → undeliverable), not VAT law. Pitching "input VAT at risk because of wrong buyer address" would overstate it; minor buyer-address errors are an old problem (Abschn. 15.2a UStAE) and missing BT-10 is VAT-irrelevant per BMF.
- 2027 (large issuers) and 2028 (everyone) are the forcing dates: every German B2B supplier will need, for every customer, a validated electronic delivery address by then. That is a one-time collection wave plus ongoing maintenance — a plausible wedge for a "request" (templated data request) product.

## 2. ViDA (from 2030) and VAT ID validation (VIES / BZSt qualified confirmation)

### Takeaway
ViDA (adopted 11 Mar 2025, in force 14 Apr 2025) lets member states mandate domestic e-invoicing without derogation, and from 1 July 2030 makes EN 16931 e-invoicing plus near-real-time digital reporting mandatory for intra-EU B2B supplies, with a 10-day issuance deadline — so buyer VAT IDs must be correct at the time of invoicing, and the supplier's bank account becomes a mandatory invoice field. VAT-ID validation (VIES/BZSt qualified confirmation, which checks name, town, postcode and street against the registry) is already the German good-faith safeguard for tax-exempt intra-EU supplies.

### Cited Findings
- Council adopted ViDA on 11 March 2025; published in OJ 25 March 2025; in force 14 April 2025 — [Banqup](https://www.banqup.com/resources/blog/vida-adopted-everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-new-e-invoicing-and-reporting-requirements); [Meijburg & Co](https://www.meijburg.com/news/eu-proposal-vat-digital-age-package-formally-adopted)
- EU Commission milestones: 14 Apr 2025 member states can introduce mandatory domestic e-invoicing; 1 Jan 2027 OSS/IOSS clarifications; 1 Jul 2028 platform deemed-supplier and single VAT registration measures; 1 Jul 2030 Digital Reporting Requirements for cross-border B2B; 1 Jan 2035 national DRR systems must converge to the EU model — [European Commission, ViDA](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-digital-age-vida_en)
- Directive (EU) 2025/516 from 1 Jul 2030: e-invoice = structured format (PDF no longer qualifies); EN 16931 as the standard (other standards only domestically); issuance of a compliant e-invoice "shall not be subject to acceptance by the recipient"; intra-EU invoices due "no later than 10 days following the chargeable event" (was: 15th of following month); new mandatory Art. 226 fields include "the supplier's bank account numbers, virtual account numbers or other identifiers of the accounts into which the invoice can be paid" — [Invoicerr summary quoting Directive 2025/516](https://invoicerr.app/europe/vida/)
- DRR replaces recapitulative statements (EC Sales Lists); a new "central VIES" will hold DRR transaction data and VAT IDs, and additional data including bank details will be reported; legacy VIES phased out by July 2032 (secondary) — [Innovate Tax](https://innovatetax.com/blog/eus-vies-platform-to-be-phased-out-under-vida/); [vatcalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/eu/eu-2028-digital-reporting-requirements-drr-e-invoice/)
- BZSt qualified confirmation checks whether company name (incl. legal form), town, postcode and street associated with a foreign EU VAT ID match the registry of the member state — [Handelskammer Hamburg / BZSt via search](https://www.handelskammer-hamburg.de/recht-steuern/steuerrecht/umsatzsteuer-mehrwertsteuer/umsatzsteuer-mehrwertsteuer-international/umsatzsteuer-identifikationsnummer-6682994); [BZSt](https://www.bzst.de/DE/Unternehmen/Identifikationsnummern/Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer/AuslaendischeUSt-IdNr/auslaendische_ust_idnr_node.html)
- Qualified confirmation is a precondition for good-faith protection (Vertrauensschutz) under §6a(4) UStG for tax-exempt intra-community supplies — [Haufe](https://www.haufe.de/id/beitrag/umsatzsteuer-identifikationsnummer-4-bestaetigungsverfahren-HI856337.html); [REB Steuerberatung](https://reb-steuerberatung.de/insights/qualifizierte-bestaetigungsanfrage-bzst-ust-idnr-pruefen.html)
- BZSt channels: online form for single queries; a new REST API for automated checks from business software; the XML-RPC interface became obsolete on 30 Nov 2025; nightly maintenance 23:00–05:00 — [BZSt: Bestätigung ausländischer USt-IdNrn](https://www.bzst.de/DE/Unternehmen/Identifikationsnummern/Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer/AuslaendischeUSt-IdNr/auslaendische_ust_idnr_node.html)
- BZSt confirms only *foreign* EU VAT IDs to German businesses via this service; domestic IDs have a separate procedure — [BZSt](https://www.bzst.de/DE/Unternehmen/Identifikationsnummern/Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer/AuslaendischeUSt-IdNr/auslaendische_ust_idnr_node.html); [BZSt inländische](https://www.bzst.de/DE/Unternehmen/Identifikationsnummern/Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer/InlaendischeUSt-IdNr/inlaendische_ust_idnr_node.html)

### Inferences
- A VAT ID shared by the customer through a living grant is a *claim*; the authoritative check stays VIES/BZSt. A product in this space should pair the customer-provided value with an automated BZSt REST qualified confirmation (and keep the result as evidence) rather than replace it. The grant's value is freshness and provenance (who asserted it, when), VIES provides registry truth.
- ViDA makes the *supplier's* IBAN a mandatory invoice field from 2030 for intra-EU invoices, which increases the attack surface for invoice-redirection: an altered e-invoice carries a machine-readable fake IBAN. A verified, owner-controlled IBAN source becomes more relevant, not less.
- The 10-day issuance deadline and real-time reporting leave no slack for chasing a customer's current VAT ID/address after the fact.

## 3. How often B2B master data goes stale, and the cost of wrong data (incl. payment-redirection fraud)

### Takeaway
Hard, primary, Germany-specific statistics on how often customer billing master data changes are scarce; the widely quoted "B2B data decays ~20–30% per year" figures come from Dun & Bradstreet marketing and data vendors and mostly concern contact persons, not company billing data. The fraud side is much better documented: Allianz Trade reports supplier/customer-impersonation schemes growing sharply in Germany in 2025 (Bestellerbetrug cases +61%, damage +139%; fake-president damage +81%), UK Finance counts £41.3m invoice/mandate-scam losses in 2025, and the FBI reports $3.04bn BEC losses in 2025.

### Cited Findings
Staleness / data quality
- D&B figures (via secondary sources): B2B contact data decays ~2.1%/month, 22–30%/year (other citations: 30–40%/year); "every 30 minutes, 120 company addresses change, 75 phone numbers are updated, 20 CEOs leave their jobs" — [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/insights/whats-the-average-rate-of-data-decay-in-a-b2b-contact-database-and-how-do-i-address-it); [Airscale](https://airscale.io/blog/b2b-contact-data-decays-faster-than-you-think-here-is-the-evidence); [D&B 2017 B2B marketing data report](https://www.dnb.com/content/dam/english/dnb-solutions/sales-and-marketing/2017-b2b-marketing-datareport-dnb.pdf) — note: old (2017) and marketing-oriented; mostly contact-person decay, US-centric.
- Gartner: poor data quality costs organizations at least $12.9m per year on average (Gartner 2020, derived from 154 reference customers of data-quality vendors — i.e. large enterprises) — [Gartner Data Quality](https://www.gartner.com/en/data-analytics/topics/data-quality); [eminence.ch on the $12.9M figure's origin](https://eminence.ch/en/cost-poor-data-quality/)
- Creditreform tracks relocations (Umzüge) and re-namings (Umfirmierungen) as part of company history, with >1,000 researchers maintaining company data — showing company identity data changes are frequent enough to be a paid monitoring product — [Creditreform Unternehmensinformationen](https://www.creditreform.de/loesungen/bonitaet-risikobewertung/unternehmensinformationen)
- Practitioner claim: correcting a faulty paper invoice costs on average $53.50; misdirected payments (6%) often trace to low master data quality (secondary, unsourced figures within article) — [averdes](https://www.averdes.com/fehler-in-der-kreditorenbuchhaltung-in-chancen-verwandeln/); [Haufe: Datenqualität im Rechnungswesen](https://haufe.de/finance/haufe-finance-office-premium/digitalisierung-im-rechnungswesen-hohe-datenqualitaet-1-warum-ist-datenqualitaet-im-rechnungswesen-bedeutsam_idesk_PI20354_HI14196972.html)

Fraud — Germany
- Allianz Trade loss statistics (published 20 Jan 2026): social-engineering fraud cases +60% in 2025; Bestellerbetrug (ordering fraud) cases +61% and damages +139% in 2025, displacing Zahlungsbetrug (payment diversion) as the most common social-engineering scheme; fake-president damages +200% in 2024 and +81% in 2025 while case counts fell (−12% 2024, −13% 2025); average fake-president damage in the single-digit millions, large cases double-digit millions; insiders caused 60% of cases and 65% of damages in 2025 — [Allianz Trade press release 20.01.2026](https://www.allianz-trade.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/alarmstufe-ki-schaeden-durch-fake-president-betrug-vervielfachen-sich.html)
- Allianz Trade definitions: "Zahlungsbetrug (Payment Diversion)": payment flows are redirected through manipulated invoices or messages with an allegedly changed bank account; "Bestellerbetrug": fraudsters pose as a *customer* and redirect goods to manipulated delivery addresses — [Allianz Trade via search](https://www.allianz-trade.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/alarmstufe-ki-schaeden-durch-fake-president-betrug-vervielfachen-sich.html); [Allianz Trade Bestellerbetrug](https://www.allianz-trade.de/vertrauensschadenversicherung/bestellerbetrug.html)
- Earlier Allianz Trade (published 20 Sep 2023): Zahlungsbetrug 2022 vs 2021 cases +29%, damages +33%; 2023 YTD cases +15%, damages +21% — [Allianz Trade 2023](https://www.allianz-trade.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/allianz-trade-statistik-fake-president-betrug-kommt-wieder-in-mode.html)
- BKA Bundeslagebild Cybercrime 2025 (published May 2026): ~335,000 cybercrime cases (narrow sense) registered in 2025; estimated damage to the German economy EUR 202.4bn (~4.5% GDP — this figure is the Bitkom economic-damage estimate cited by BKA) — [BKA Bundeslagebild Cybercrime 2025](https://www.bka.de/BLBCybercrime2025); [BMI press release](https://www.bmi.bund.de/SharedDocs/pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/05/lagebild-cybercrime.html)
- A BKA 2024 research report put German BEC damage at just under EUR 110m (secondary, via a law-firm blog; not verified in the primary) — [anwalt.de](https://www.anwalt.de/rechtstipps/anwalt-bec-und-ceo-betrug-krypto-diebstahl-und-phishing-und-millionen-die-ueber-gefaelschte-e-mails-verschwinden-271312.html)

Fraud — UK/US benchmarks
- UK Finance Annual Fraud Report 2026 (covering 2025): invoice and mandate scam losses £41.3m (−4% y/y), 2,305 cases (−2%); invoice/mandate, CEO fraud and impersonation losses at record lows attributed to industry prevention; total APP fraud losses £576.4m (+19%), of which £354.3m (61%) reimbursed — [UK Finance Fraud Report 2026 press release](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/news-and-insight/press-release/fraud-report-2026-press-release); [UK Finance report PDF](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/system/files/2026-06/UK%20Finance%20Fraud%20Report%202026.pdf)
- UK Finance definition: in an invoice or mandate scam the victim tries to pay a legitimate payee but the criminal convinces them to redirect the payment to an account they control, incl. posing as a supplier — [UK Finance 2025 report](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/policy-and-guidance/reports-and-publications/annual-fraud-report-2025)
- FBI IC3 2025: BEC losses $3.046bn (2024: $2.77bn), ~24,768 complaints, average >$122k per complaint; 86% of BEC funds moved via wire/ACH; total cyber-enabled losses $20.877bn (+26%) — [FBI IC3 2025 report](https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf); [Red Sift summary](https://redsift.com/blog/fbi-ic3-2025-report-email-fraud)
- Trustpair (US, vendor with commercial interest): 69% of companies targeted by vendor fraud in 2024 (47% prior year); only 32% validate vendor bank accounts continuously; 2026 report: invoice fraud most common attack (58%) — [Trustpair 2025 report press release](https://trustpair.com/blog/fraud-report-2025-press-release/); [Trustpair 2026 trends](https://trustpair.com/blog/2026-fraud-trends/)

### Inferences
- The staleness argument should be framed around *events* (moves, re-namings/mergers, bank switches, new AP inbox, new e-invoicing channel) rather than the generic D&B "30% decay" figures, which are about contact people in sales databases. No trustworthy German statistic on billing-data change frequency was found.
- Allianz Trade's "Bestellerbetrug" (fraudsters posing as a customer to redirect *deliveries*) is directly about *customer* master data (delivery/billing address changes), and it was the fastest-growing social-engineering scheme in Germany in 2025. This is arguably a stronger hook for a supplier-side "customer data via verified grant" product than invoice redirection, which targets the supplier's own IBAN as seen by the customer.
- The UK decline in invoice/mandate losses is attributed to industry controls (Confirmation of Payee etc.), which supports the thesis that verified payee data works; the EU's Verification of Payee (see §6) is the analogue.

- No primary German/EU survey found quantifying frequency of customer address/IBAN/invoice-email changes per year.
- No German statistic separating "fake bank-detail change" (Zahlungsbetrug) losses in EUR; Allianz Trade publishes only percentage changes.
- Primary BKA 2024 BEC figure not verified.

## 4. How supplier/customer master data management works today; lightweight options

### Takeaway
Enterprise tooling solves this for the *buyer's supplier* data: supplier portals (SAP Ariba/Business Network, Coupa, Jaggaer) let suppliers self-maintain their master data, and bolt-on verifiers (Trustpair, Relish) check bank-account ownership via banking data, open banking or call-centres. Nothing comparable and lightweight exists for SMEs/freelancers, and almost nothing addresses the reverse direction (a supplier keeping its *customers'* billing data current); SMBs rely on e-mail, Excel and occasional credit-bureau (Creditreform, D&B) lookups.

### Cited Findings
- Supplier portals let suppliers maintain their own master data via profile or change request, reducing the buyer's maintenance effort (German practitioner sources) — [ecratum: Lieferanten-Stammdaten für KMU](https://blog.ecratum.de/lieferanten-stammdaten-warum-eine-gute-datenqualitaet-gerade-fuer-kmu-wichtig-ist); [HCOB Lieferantenportal](https://www.hcob-bank.com/lieferantenportal/)
- SAP Ariba provides syntax validation, visibility and optionality settings for supplier bank account information (i.e. format checks, not ownership) — [SAP Help](https://help.sap.com/docs/ARIBA_SOURCING/c6163e943b0d48e0885ac73047145cbf/e6c10e0f6bec4e57b6515efffd8436db.html)
- Trustpair integrates via API into SAP Ariba/S/4HANA to verify supplier bank details at onboarding and throughout the lifecycle using banking data sources, replacing manual callback procedures; it is listed as an SAP partner solution — [Trustpair for SAP Ariba](https://trustpair.com/connect/sap-ariba/); [SAP Store listing](https://www.sap.com/products/spend-management/partners/trustpair-trustpair-vendor-fraud-detection-for-sap-ariba.html)
- Relish "Data Assure" is Coupa BSM Platform Ready and an SAP Endorsed App; its bank-account-ownership check falls back to bank-document automation, open banking or a call centre — [Relish](https://relishiq.com/blog/supplier-bank-account-verification-tools/)
- Recommended controls for vendor bank changes: dual approval, never accept bank changes via e-mail alone, 48-hour cooling-off, segregation of duties, automated master-file change alerts, verification before first payment (vendor/consultant guidance) — [MonitorPay](https://monitorpay.ai/supplier-bank-account-change-fraud-how-to-spot-it-stop-it-and-recover-lost-payments/); [Relish](https://relishiq.com/blog/supplier-validation-tools-coupa-sap/)
- An open-source SAP vendor portal project has an open security issue: an approved supplier can change its payout bank account with no re-verification and no audit entry — an illustration of the gap in naive self-service portals — [GitHub issue](https://github.com/sage-manas/sap_vendor_portal/issues/53)
- Creditreform and D&B sell company information including master data, register entries, management, relocations and re-namings, sourced from trade registers and insolvency notices — [Creditreform](https://www.creditreform.de/loesungen/bonitaet-risikobewertung/unternehmensinformationen)
- Media report (Sept 2026) of a Basware–Trustpair merger, signalling consolidation of e-invoicing networks and vendor-verification tooling (secondary, unverified) — [hostmerchantservices.com](https://hostmerchantservices.com/2026/09/vendor-impersonation-fraud/)

### Inferences
- Portals are buyer-centric (the big buyer forces its suppliers onto its portal); each supplier ends up maintaining the same data in N customer portals. A grant model inverts this: the data owner keeps one record and grants it to N counterparties. That is a genuine structural difference, but it needs network effects (both sides using it) to beat "send us an e-mail".
- Credit bureaus cover registry facts (legal name, seat, register number) but not operational billing data (AP e-mail, Peppol ID, PO/buyer reference, IBAN), which only the company itself knows. That operational slice is where a customer-owned, living record adds value.
- No lightweight SMB/freelancer tool for "keep my customers' billing data current" was found in this research; absence of evidence, not proof.

### Gaps
- Did not verify SAP MDG / Coupa / Jaggaer product docs directly (their supplier self-service and bank-change workflows).
- Did not find German SMB-focused "Stammdaten-Selbstauskunft" SaaS offerings to benchmark pricing.

## 5. APIs/webhooks of invoicing tools popular in Germany (can a third party update a contact; are there change events?)

### Takeaway
Lexware Office (lexoffice) and Invoice Ninja both allow creating/updating contacts via API and emit contact-change webhooks, making them the most practical first integration targets; easybill has a REST API with customer endpoints and a webhook API; sevDesk allows contact updates but (per a third-party integrator) has no webhooks. Crater, Kimai and FastBill were not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Lexware Office API: contacts created via POST and updated via PUT ("Update an existing contact with id {id} with the data given in the payload"); fields include billing/shipping addresses (one of each per update), e-mail addresses and phone numbers by type, `vatRegistrationId` ("has to follow the german rules"), and XRechnung fields `buyerReference` (Leitweg-ID) and `vendorNumberAtCustomer`; no IBAN/bank fields on contacts; contacts with multiple entries per address/e-mail list cannot be updated via API; optimistic locking via `version`; rate limit 2 requests/second (HTTP 429) — [Lexware API docs](https://developers.lexware.io/docs/)
- Lexware Office event subscriptions: register an HTTPS callback; events include `contact.created`, `contact.changed`/`contact.updated` and `contact.deleted` (exact names: Lexware docs list "Event Types"; third-party summaries give contact.created/updated/deleted) — [Lexware API docs](https://developers.lexware.io/docs/); [API Evangelist profile](https://github.com/api-evangelist/lexoffice); [Apideck connector](https://developers.apideck.com/apis/accounting/lexware-office/overview)
- lexoffice was rebranded "Lexware Office" in 2025 (Haufe Group) — [API Evangelist profile](https://github.com/api-evangelist/lexoffice)
- sevDesk API: `PUT /Contact/{id}` updates a contact; addresses via separate ContactAddress API — [sevdesk API](https://api.sevdesk.de/); [PHP client ContactApi docs](https://github.com/Pommespanzer/sevdesk-php-client/blob/master/docs/Api/ContactApi.md); [ContactAddressApi](https://github.com/Pommespanzer/sevdesk-php-client/blob/master/docs/Api/ContactAddressApi.md)
- sevDesk: "publishes no webhooks", no push notifications — changes must be polled (integrator blog, secondary); new API pagination limits announced 11 Mar 2025, enforced by 30 May 2025 — [Maesn: sevdesk API](https://www.maesn.com/blog/how-to-integrate-sevdesk-api); [sevDesk Tech Blog API News](https://tech.sevdesk.com/api_news/)
- Invoice Ninja v5 (open source, self-hostable): REST API for clients; webhooks POST the full entity JSON with `event_id`; event constants include EVENT_CREATE_CLIENT (1), EVENT_UPDATE_CLIENT (10), EVENT_DELETE_CLIENT (11), EVENT_ARCHIVE_CLIENT (37), EVENT_RESTORE_CLIENT (45), plus vendor create/update/delete (5/13/14) — [Invoice Ninja source, app/Models/Webhook.php](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/invoiceninja/invoiceninja/v5-stable/app/Models/Webhook.php); [Invoice Ninja API reference](https://api-docs.invoicing.co/)
- easybill: REST API (base https://api.easybill.de/rest/v1) covering customers, contacts, documents, payments, and a Webhook API (webhook model: events array, secret, url, is_active); exact event names (e.g. customer.update) not verified — [easybill API](https://www.easybill.de/api/); [easybill WebhookApi docs (generated client)](https://github.com/cbeyersdorf/easybill/blob/main/docs/Api/WebhookApi.md); [CustomerApi](https://github.com/cbeyersdorf/easybill/blob/master/docs/Api/CustomerApi.md)

### Inferences
- A "revoked → invoicing tool" sync is technically feasible: on grant update, PUT the contact (Lexware: respect `version`, 2 req/s; sevDesk: PUT Contact + ContactAddress). The reverse direction (detect that the supplier's local copy diverged) works via Lexware/Invoice Ninja webhooks, but needs polling for sevDesk.
- Lexware contacts carry `buyerReference` (Leitweg-ID) and VAT ID but not bank details; the mapping of a customer record to an invoicing contact is mostly address + VAT ID + invoice e-mail + buyer reference. Peppol participant ID / BT-49 storage per contact in these tools was not confirmed.
- Invoice Ninja being self-hosted/open-source aligns culturally with a self-hosted revoked and has the richest client events; a good first reference integration.

### Gaps
- Kimai (time tracking, has invoicing), Crater, FastBill: API/webhook capabilities not researched.
- Whether Lexware/sevDesk/easybill contacts have a dedicated field for the buyer's Peppol ID / e-invoice delivery address was not confirmed.
- Lexware API plan restrictions (which Lexware Office tiers include API access) not confirmed.

## 6. Is bank-detail-change fraud a strong argument for cryptographically verified, owner-controlled bank details?

### Takeaway
Yes, directionally: the dominant fraud patterns (payment diversion via "changed bank details", fake-president, customer impersonation to redirect deliveries) all exploit unauthenticated change requests over e-mail, and the recommended controls (never accept bank changes by e-mail alone, out-of-band verification, dual approval) describe exactly an authenticated, owner-controlled update channel. But incumbents already sell bank-account *ownership* verification (Trustpair, Relish, and payee-name checks at banks), so a signature proves *who* asserted the IBAN, not that the account belongs to them — it complements rather than replaces ownership checks.

### Cited Findings
- Allianz Trade defines payment diversion as redirecting payments through manipulated invoices or messages "mit angeblich geänderter Bankverbindung"; Zahlungsbetrug was the most common social-engineering scheme until overtaken by Bestellerbetrug in 2025 — [Allianz Trade 20.01.2026](https://www.allianz-trade.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/alarmstufe-ki-schaeden-durch-fake-president-betrug-vervielfachen-sich.html)
- Allianz Trade attributes the growth to AI making it easier to deceive employees into transfers — [Allianz Trade 20.01.2026](https://www.allianz-trade.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/alarmstufe-ki-schaeden-durch-fake-president-betrug-vervielfachen-sich.html); [CRN 2026](https://www.crn.de/news/2026/cyberbetrug-allianz-trade-lost-ki-alarm-aus)
- FBI IC3 2025: $3.046bn BEC losses, 86% via wire/ACH — fast and often unrecoverable — [FBI IC3 2025](https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf)
- Best-practice controls: prohibit accepting bank changes via e-mail alone; dual approval; 48h cooling-off; automated verification of any new/changed bank account before first payment — [MonitorPay](https://monitorpay.ai/supplier-bank-account-change-fraud-how-to-spot-it-stop-it-and-recover-lost-payments/)
- Only 32% of (US) companies validate vendor bank accounts continuously (vendor survey) — [Trustpair](https://trustpair.com/blog/fraud-report-2025-press-release/)
- Incumbent approach to ownership: bank data sources, open banking, bank-document automation, call-centre verification — [Relish](https://relishiq.com/blog/supplier-bank-account-verification-tools/); [Trustpair](https://trustpair.com/connect/sap-ariba/)
- UK invoice/mandate scam losses fell to record lows (£41.3m, 2025), attributed to industry prevention investment — [UK Finance 2026](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/news-and-insight/press-release/fraud-report-2026-press-release)
- ViDA adds the supplier's bank account identifiers as a mandatory invoice field from 1 Jul 2030 — [Invoicerr on Directive 2025/516](https://invoicerr.app/europe/vida/)

### Inferences
- The strongest framing for revoked: "bank details and billing data change only through an authenticated channel the owner controls, and every consumer sees the change with provenance" — this directly closes the "e-mail says our bank details changed" vector and the Bestellerbetrug "customer says our delivery address changed" vector. DNS-anchored identities give the recipient a way to check that the asserting party is the domain it claims to be.
- Limits: (1) A compromised owner account/device or a malicious insider (Allianz Trade: insiders ~60% of cases, 65% of damage in 2025) still produces a "verified" wrong IBAN; (2) a signature does not prove account ownership, so pairing with the EU Verification of Payee (IBAN–name check mandated for SEPA credit transfers under the Instant Payments Regulation, applicable from Oct 2025 — from background knowledge, not verified in this session) or an ownership check is prudent; (3) it only helps if the payer refuses changes arriving any other way — the product must make "we only accept bank changes via the grant" an enforceable policy.
- Commercial angle: fraud prevention is a budget line (Vertrauensschadenversicherung, Trustpair/Relish pricing) that "data freshness" is not; the fraud argument is likely the better sales hook, while e-invoicing (2027/2028) is the better timing hook.

### Gaps
- Did not retrieve primary sources on the EU Verification of Payee (Regulation (EU) 2024/886) and its effect on invoice-redirection fraud in 2025/2026; flagged as background knowledge only.
- No German-language BSI publication specific to bank-detail-change fraud was retrieved.
- No evidence found of any existing product using cryptographic signatures (vs. bank-data checks) for supplier/customer bank details.
